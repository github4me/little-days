# Table-inspired play review — 2026-09-08

The photographed checklist has no age or technique guidance. It is an idea list,
not an authority for clinical advice. App age ranges and short session lengths
are editorial browsing aids, not developmental deadlines or prescribed doses.

## Added

| Activity | Browsing age (completed months) | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gentle touch / 轻柔抚触 | 0 to under 12 | [NHS infant massage](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/infant-massage/): gentle touch and parent–baby interaction. No treatment claims or joint stretching. |
| A gentle rattle / 听听小摇铃 | 4 to under 12 | [CDC four-month activities](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/4-months.html): safe, easy-grip toys such as rattles. |
| Little kicks / 小脚自由踢 | 4 to under 9 | [CDC four-month activities](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/4-months.html): opportunities to kick and explore. A kick piano is optional, not a required purchase or therapy. |
| Mirror smiles / 镜子里的笑脸 | 6 to under 18 | [CDC six-month guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/6-months.html): interest in mirrors, shared attention and responding to baby. |

Each has bilingual instructions and safety notes, and participates in the same
age-based defaults, manual selection, cross-age warning and daily check-ins.

## Already covered

Black-and-white cards, supervised awake tummy time, reaching/grasping, texture
exploration, eye contact and conversation, books, songs, peekaboo and floor
movement already have cards. Washing (including the face) and bathing already
have care records. No duplicate tasks or compulsory daily targets were added.

## Not added from this table alone

Passive exercise and gas-relief routines need clearer technique and individual
context. Pulling a baby into sitting is not equivalent to supported sitting;
[AAP guidance on pulled elbow](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/orthopedic/Pages/Nursemaids-Elbow.aspx)
warns against pulling or swinging children by their hands/arms.
“Vestibular training,” head-butting games and infant swimming are not sufficiently
specified here for generic instructions. These omissions do not imply all
clinician-guided movement or suitable swimming programs are unsafe.

Peekaboo uses the adult's hands over the adult's face, not cloth over the baby.
No neck floats, forced movements, spinning, shaking or treatment promises are
introduced. Families can ask their care team about individual exercise needs.
